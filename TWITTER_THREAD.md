## Tweet 2 (The Problem)
Construction material suppliers face a massive problem:

❌ Customers call asking "How much for 50m³ of gravel?"
❌ Managers spend 15-20 mins per quote
❌ Need to calculate: distance, truck capacity, pricing tiers
❌ Customers wait... and often buy elsewhere

---

## Tweet 3 (The Pain Point)
The real cost?

• 20+ calls per day = 6+ hours on calculations
• 30% of customers abandon before getting a quote
• Manual errors in distance/pricing calculations
• No transparency = low customer trust

There had to be a better way.

---

## Tweet 4 (The Solution - Overview)
We built an automated delivery calculator that:

✅ Calculates in 2 minutes (not 20)
✅ Finds nearest supplier automatically
✅ Shows route on map with distance
✅ Adjusts quantity for truck capacity
✅ Transparent price breakdown

Mobile-first. No app download needed.

---

## Tweet 5 (How It Works)
The flow is dead simple:

1️⃣ Customer selects material (gravel, sand, etc.)
2️⃣ Chooses transport type (20m³ or 30m³ truck)
3️⃣ Enters delivery address
4️⃣ System calculates everything automatically
5️⃣ Shows total price + breakdown

All in under 2 minutes. ⚡

---

## Tweet 6 (The Smart Part)
The magic happens behind the scenes:

🔍 Finds closest supplier with that material
📍 Calculates exact route using Google Maps
⚖️ Converts volume to weight (density tables)
🚛 Rounds quantity to match truck capacity
💰 Applies correct pricing tier by distance

Customer sees final price. Clean. Transparent.

---

## Tweet 7 (Example Calculation)
Real example:

Customer needs: 50m³ granite gravel
Location: 65 km from supplier

System calculates:
• Rounds to 60m³ (2 trucks × 30m³)
• Weight: 82.8 tons
• Material: $2,340
• Delivery: $287
• TOTAL: $2,627

Customer sees route on map + full breakdown.
